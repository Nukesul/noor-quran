# Noor Quran — North Star

## Our Mission

To create the most peaceful Quran experience ever made.

---

## We believe

The Quran should be the first thing people see.

The interface should disappear.

Reading should feel effortless.

Beauty should support reading, never compete with it.

Technology should help, not distract.

---

## Every feature must answer:

Does this help someone read the Quran better?

If not,

don't build it.

---

## Our design principles

Calm.

Simple.

Warm.

Elegant.

Focused.

Fast.

Accessible.

Offline-first.

---

## Our promise

No advertisements.

No clutter.

No unnecessary steps.

No distractions.

Only what truly matters.
